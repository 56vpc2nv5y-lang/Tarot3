# 🐱 Cat Whispers — 全78张塔罗牌 Gemini 生成Prompt

## 使用方法
1. 先发「风格锚定」建立对话基础
2. 每张牌单独发一条Prompt（前面加 "Continue the established style:"）
3. 若Gemini拒绝，把 "tarot" 改成 "oracle card"，把敏感牌名改成括号里的替代词

---

## 【必须先发】风格锚定 Style Anchor

```
You are creating a complete series of 78 mystical oracle card illustrations 
called "Cat Whispers." 

Consistent style rules for every card:
- Medium: soft watercolor with clean ink outlines
- All humanoid figures are replaced by anthropomorphic cats with 
  expressive semi-realistic faces, wearing period-appropriate clothing
- Palette: warm, slightly desaturated — rose pinks, creams, soft golds, 
  muted teals. Nothing neon or overly saturated
- Mood: whimsical but emotionally resonant, not childish
- Format: portrait orientation, 2:3.5 ratio
- White card border, small empty space at bottom for card title text
- Each card has its own distinct composition but feels part of the same series
- Backgrounds are detailed but never compete with the main subject
- Cat expressions carry the emotional weight of the scene

Maintain these rules for every card in this series.
```

在同一对话连续生成时，最前面加：
`"Continue the Cat Whispers oracle card series (same watercolor+ink style, cat characters, warm palette, white border)"`

- "Death" → "Transformation card showing cycles of change"
- "The Devil" → "Shadow card showing comfortable chains"
- "Three of Swords" → "Heartbreak card showing emotional pain"
- "Ten of Swords" → "Rock bottom card showing dramatic endings"

**批量生成建议：**
先生成 Major Arcana 22张，确认风格统一后再做 Minor Arcana。
每次生成后保存图片，命名规则：`cat_[suit]_[number/name].png`
例：`cat_major_00_fool.png`，`cat_wands_01_ace.png`
