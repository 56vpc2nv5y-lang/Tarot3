## 【必须先发】风格锚定 Style Anchor

```
You are creating a complete series of 78 mystical oracle cards called 
"Chinese Divine" (东方神明). Every card reimagines classic tarot archetypes 
through the lens of Chinese mythology and classical art.

Consistent style rules for every card:
- Medium: Song Dynasty gongbi (工笔画) technique meets contemporary 
  Chinese illustration — precise brushwork, rich jewel-toned colors, 
  gold accent lines on details
- Characters are drawn from Chinese mythology, folklore, and history
- Color palette: cinnabar red, imperial gold, jade green, ink black, 
  cloud white, celestial blue. Rich, saturated, never flat
- Atmospheric elements: clouds and mist (祥云), celestial light rays, 
  lotus flowers, lanterns, incense smoke
- Format: portrait orientation, 2:3.5 ratio
- White card border, bottom space shows Chinese title + English subtitle
- Top center: small circular seal stamp motif
- Bottom right corner: small red seal stamp (朱砂印)
- Compositions feel monumental and sacred — this is divine art

Maintain these rules for every card in this series.
```
